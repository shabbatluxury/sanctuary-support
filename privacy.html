Privacy Policy

App: Sanctuary (ShabbatLuxury) for iPhone
Developer: Yevgeniy Yampolskiy
Effective date: October 12, 2026
Contact: shabbatluxury.sanctuary@gmail.com

Summary

Sanctuary shows Shabbat candle-lighting and Havdalah times for your location. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer does not operate any servers and does not collect or store your personal data.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your iPhone's location to calculate times for where you are and to show the direction of Jerusalem (compass). You can instead choose a city or ZIP code manually. You can change the location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

When you choose a city manually, the text you type is converted to coordinates using Apple's geocoding service, which is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data sent to Hebcal

To obtain Shabbat and zmanim times, the app sends your approximate coordinates and time zone to the public Hebcal API at hebcal.com. Your IP address is necessarily visible to Hebcal as part of this request. Hebcal states that it keeps server logs for a short period and does not sell personal data; see the Hebcal Privacy Policy. The app sends no name, email, device identifier or account information.

If the Hebcal service is unreachable, the app calculates approximate sunset-based times on the device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example 15 and 30 minutes before candle lighting) on your device. These are generated on the device; no push-notification server is used and no notification data leaves your phone.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (reminder switches, chosen city) are stored locally on your iPhone in the app's storage. They are not sent to the developer.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No user accounts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
